Delete your Oathway Canada account and data

Oathway Canada, by Oathway

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Oathway Canada and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm. If Google asks you to choose your account again, do so. This confirms it's you.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email vinith0140@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the Google email address you used to sign in. If that isn't possible, tell us that address. We'll confirm the account is yours, delete it within 30 days, and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Google name, email, profile photo link, account ID).
  • All saved progress: name, test date, province, settings, scores and exam history, mastered/missed/saved questions, checklist, streak and passed date.
  • Problem reports you sent.
  • The app's data on your phone (deleting in the app clears it right away).

Deleted data leaves our systems immediately. Google removes it from its backups within about 180 days.

What is kept

  • Purchase record (product, transaction ID and date): kept by RevenueCat and Google Play as long as needed to verify the purchase and for tax and accounting. Deleting your account doesn't refund a purchase. You can manage purchases and refunds in Google Play.

Signing out is not the same as deleting. Sign-out clears your phone but keeps your progress in your account.
