Oathway Canada Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Oathway ("we", "us") makes Oathway Canada, an app for studying for the Canadian citizenship test. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who handles it, and your choices. Oathway Canada is not affiliated with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

The short version

  • You sign in with Google so your progress is saved to your account and follows you to a new phone.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and your saved progress in the app at any time.

What we collect and why

WhatWhy
Your Google account basics: name, email address, profile photo link, and an account ID created by FirebaseTo sign you in and keep your progress in your account
What you enter: the name you'd like us to call you (optional), your test date (optional), your province or territory, and your settings (voice, reading speed)To personalize the app. Your province decides which provincial questions you see
Your study progress: scores and exam history, questions you've mastered, missed or saved, checklist ticks, study streak, and the date you passed (if you add it)To save your progress and restore it when you sign in on another phone
Problem reports you send: question number, reason, optional note, your account ID, and the timeTo fix questions. Only we can read reports
Voice problems (no account link): if a voice recording can't play, the app sends a short note: the language, which recording, the kind of problem, the app version and your phone's system (Android or iOS and its version). At most one note per recording per dayTo find and fix broken recordings. These notes don't include your account ID, name or email
Purchase details: which product you bought, the transaction ID and date, and whether the unlock is active, linked to your account IDTo unlock the full app and restore your purchase. Google Play handles payment. We never see your card details

What we don't collect: your phone's location, contacts, photos or files, microphone or camera, or advertising ID. The app has no ads and no analytics or crash-tracking tools. The only automatic report is the short voice-problem note above.

Voice tutor: the tutor's voice is a set of recordings that the app downloads from our audio server (run by Cloudflare) the first time each one plays, and then keeps on your phone. Like any website, the server sees your internet (IP) address and which recording was asked for; we don't link this to your account. Anything not recorded is read by your phone's built-in text-to-speech, which belongs to your phone and follows your phone's settings. The app never records you.

Ratings: the app may show Google Play's rating window. Google handles your rating. We only see a review if you post one publicly.

Who handles your information

We don't sell your personal information or share it with anyone for advertising. These service providers handle it only to run the app for us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) handles sign-in and stores your progress and reports. To keep sign-in secure and prevent abuse, Firebase also processes your IP address and basic device information.
  • Cloudflare hosts the voice recordings. It sees your IP address when a recording downloads, as any website does.
  • RevenueCat checks and restores your purchase. It receives your account ID and purchase details.
  • Google Play processes payments and the rating window under Google's Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Where it is stored

  • Study progress, problem reports and voice-problem notes: Google Cloud in Montréal, Canada.
  • Sign-in records: Firebase Authentication in the United States.
  • Purchase records: RevenueCat in the United States.
  • A copy of your progress is also kept on your phone.

Information stored outside Canada is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessed by its courts or authorities.

How long we keep it

  • Account and progress: until you delete your account. Deleted data leaves our live systems right away. Google removes it from its backups within about 180 days.
  • Problem reports: until you delete your account, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Voice-problem notes: no longer than 12 months. They aren't linked to your account, so deleting your account doesn't find them; they contain nothing that identifies you.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to verify your purchase and to meet our tax and accounting obligations.
  • On your phone: until you sign out, delete your account, or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your saved progress, your problem reports and your sign-in account, and clears the app's data from your phone. Signing out also clears the phone but keeps your progress in your account.

No app? Email vinith0140@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, and we'll delete your account within 30 days. Details: https://vinith0140.github.io/oathway-canada-site/delete-account.html.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA and, for Quebec residents, Quebec's privacy law, you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • withdraw your consent.

Most of your information you can see and change in the app. Withdrawing consent means deleting your account, because saving progress needs sign-in. Email vinith0140@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. If you aren't satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Your saved progress can be read only by your own signed-in account, and problem reports only by us. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to protect.

Children

Oathway Canada is meant for adults preparing for the citizenship test. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has signed in, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the app.

Contact
